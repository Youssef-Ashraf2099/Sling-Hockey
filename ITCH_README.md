## 🎓 How to Play

### Basic Controls
1. **Click & Drag**: Grab any puck in your territory
2. **Pull Back**: Stretch the elastic rope for power
3. **Release**: Launch the puck toward opponent's side
4. **Win Condition**: Get all your pucks on opponent's side

### Advanced Techniques
- **Angle Shots**: Pull diagonally for curved trajectories
- **Power Control**: Longer pulls = more force
- **Ricochet Shots**: Use walls for indirect attacks
- **Defensive Play**: Block opponent's shots strategically

### Pro Tips
- **Study Physics**: Each puck skin behaves differently
- **Master Timing**: Release at the right moment for accuracy
- **Use Momentum**: Chain shots for continuous pressure
- **Adapt Strategy**: Different difficulties require different approaches
